### 🏢 About OnonnoBit

**OnonnoBit** is a custom web & application development agency based in **Bogura, Bangladesh**, dedicated to building high-performance, scalable digital solutions for businesses and organizations across Bangladesh and beyond.

- 🎯 **Mission:** Delivering impactful, affordable digital solutions that empower local businesses and communities.
- 🔭 **Currently building:** Advanced web & business automation solutions.
- 🌱 **Currently exploring:** Generative AI integrations & Advanced Kotlin/Android development.
- 💬 **Ask us about:** React, Firebase, E-commerce systems, LMS platforms, or Business Automation.
- 🌍 **Reach:** 50+ projects delivered across professional, educational, and social-impact sectors.
