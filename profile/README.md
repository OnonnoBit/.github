### 🏢 About Us

**OnonnoBit** is a custom web application development agency based in **Bogura, Bangladesh**, founded and led by **Md Ridoan Mahmud Zisan**. We design and build high-performance, scalable digital products — from e-commerce platforms and learning management systems to business automation tools — helping organizations and entrepreneurs across Bangladesh grow online.

- 🎯 **Mission:** Delivering impactful, affordable, and reliable software for local and global clients.
- 🔭 **Currently building:** Advanced web solutions & AI-integrated business tools.
- 🌱 **Exploring:** Generative AI, PWA development, and Kotlin/Android apps.
- 🤝 **Open to:** Freelance projects, long-term partnerships, and collaborations.
